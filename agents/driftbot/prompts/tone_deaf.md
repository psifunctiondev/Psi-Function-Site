# Tone Deaf — {{ client.name }}

## The provocation

There is a register problem in the {{ client.category }} category.
Brands talk at the wrong emotional pitch for the moment their buyers are in.
Too clinical when buyers need empathy. Too cheerful when buyers are under
genuine pressure. Too aspirational when buyers need to justify a decision
to a skeptical CFO.

This is **Tone Deaf** — not a failure of information, but of emotional
attunement. The right claim, delivered in the wrong register, lands nowhere.

## Why it matters

{{ analysis.why_it_matters }}

Tone is not cosmetic. It signals whether a brand understands the stakes of
the buyer's situation. In categories where trust is slow to build and decisions
are high-consequence, tone is often the deciding factor between a brand that
captivates and one that gets filtered out before the first meeting.

{{ client.name }}'s buyers — {{ client.audiences | join(", ") }} — each
carry different pressures and speak different languages. A brand that meets
each of them where they actually are is rare. That rarity is an anchor point.
