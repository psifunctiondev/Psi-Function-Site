Methodos

Methodos is a framework for structuring AI-native work. Goals, tasks, and artifacts, each with a clear owner.

Designed and battle-tested inside Psi Function's own agent team, Methodos separates strategic intent from execution: who decides the goal, who builds the artifact, who keeps the knowledge. It is what makes an AI-augmented team actually run like a team.

render2image

render2image

Turn architectural renders into photo-realistic images.

render2image takes a flat Enscape (or similar) render and produces a finished photograph — same composition, same lighting, same materials — with the realism that helps clients see themselves in the space. Built on Flux and ControlNet, designed for architectural visualization studios that need client-ready imagery at the pace of design.

BrandSight

BrandSight is a structured competitive audit for brands.

Submit your website and a list of competitors; BrandSight returns a side-by-side analysis of positioning, messaging, visual identity, and customer experience — the kind of audit a strategy consultancy delivers, automated. Built on the Drift & Anchor methodology, packaged as a self-serve product.
